## Review basis and limitations

- I reviewed metadata, the first three seconds, nine evenly spaced visual samples, black-frame output, silence detection, measured loudness/true peak, and frame-similarity results for all 17 files.
- The type labels below are guesses. I did **not** watch every video end-to-end in real time, so pacing, story clarity, edit rhythm, originality, and overall taste still need human judgment. I used **NEEDS MY INPUT** where those factors could materially change the decision.
- No target-client profile or preferred work mix was supplied. These rankings therefore optimize for technical readiness and variety rather than a specific niche.
- The similarity scan found no byte-for-byte duplicates. Visual review shows that `1.mp4` and `3.mp4` are from the same shoot/series and reuse the same setup; they should not both be prominent. `16.mp4`, `20.mp4`, `21.mp4`, `24.mp4`, `25.mp4`, and `28.mp4` also appear to form one recurring creator/client cluster, so only two are featured.
- “Possible clipping / hot true peak” is a conservative warning from the measured true peak (at or above -0.1 dBTP), not proof that audible distortion exists.
